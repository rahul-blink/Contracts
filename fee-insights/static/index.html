How each fee is calculated

The definitions behind every figure here.

Satellite fee
sat_fee_applicable = fee_amt (per-unit rate) × net_qty_sold. Verified row-exact on all 86.2M rows of the extract. fee_amt alone is a rate — summing it is meaningless.
Satellite fee coverage
Two monthly extracts, one folder per month: June 2026 (30/30 days, complete) and July 2026 (30 of 31 days, and 30 July itself carries only 67% of a median day). July is therefore understated and is not comparable like-for-like against June — nothing is scaled up to hide the gap. Each month is bucketed by its own insert_ds_ist, never by a folder name, and the build errors if a month is found in more than one folder.
Satellite city
A city on the master list (satellite_cities_recovered.parquet), which carries a break-even minimum distance in km. The fee extract is not pre-filtered to this list, so every satellite view here defaults to “satellite only”.
Defect fee
₹50 × bad_returns_qty, recomputed from quantity rather than read from the extract’s penalty column, so the rate is explicit. The build reconciles the two and flags any drift.
Defect failure modes
The five buckets in the defects executive summary (handling · product quality · fulfilment · listing · authenticity) are an editorial grouping of the extract’s 14 raw complaint_type values, defined in DEFECT_BUCKETS in app.py. They are not a column in the data, and the per-type chart on that tab remains the source of truth. COMPLAINT_DAMAGED_ITEM is also visibly a catch-all — its most common phrase is “not working” — so the handling share is an upper bound.
City coordinates (map)
From GeoNames (IN populated places), matched on the master-list name with the parenthesised state as disambiguator, preferring the administrative seat then the largest population. Five spellings GeoNames does not carry (Davanagere, Kotdwar, Rajnandgaon, Sundar Nagar, Tarn Taran Sahib) go through a hand-checked alias table — never a fuzzy match, because a wrong dot is worse than a missing one. All 227 match; any that ever fail are named on the map rather than dropped. Outline: India state boundaries dissolved from district polygons and simplified to 61 KB. Boundaries are for orientation only, not an authoritative depiction.
KAM fee
kam_charges accrued once per calendar month from the month of effective_date, for contracts with kam_support = Yes. All are valid-until-cancelled, so accrual has no end date.
Recall assistance fee
recall_assistance_fee taken as reported on the corrected recall extract, which carries the money per month × manufacturer × item. It is also the one fee here that reconciles: recall_assistance_fee = chargeable_prn_qty × recall_charge_rs holds row-exact on every rated line, and the build fails loudly if that ever stops being true. prn_qty is everything returned; chargeable_prn_qty is the slice with a contract rate behind it. A line with no contract_id has no knowable fee — it is outside the chargeable set, not billed at ₹0, and about half the lines are in that state. Each line is bucketed by its own duration column, never by a filename, and deduped on (duration, manufacturer, item) so a re-pulled part cannot double-count. Supersedes the BCPL_RTV_Claim CSV drop, which applied a recall fee to returns from all 5,407 returning manufacturers with no check that a recall-assistance clause existed (₹5.66M). Joined to contracts, 260 manufacturers are chargeable and the figure is ₹1.21M.
