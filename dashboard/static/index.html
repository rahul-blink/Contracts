What is “satellite fee (applicable)”? It is net units sold × per-unit fee rate for items whose MRP meets the tier threshold — i.e. the satellite fee applicable across all satellite sales, computed regardless of contract state (Pending approval / Draft / Approved), not only where the satellite clause is finalised/aligned. Every fee figure on this dashboard is this applicable fee.
